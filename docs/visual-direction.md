# Visual direction and provenance

Experimental AI engineer portfolio: sculptural chrome, warm orange, large type, charcoal background. HG.ai, navigation anchors, project evidence, and contact destinations remain.

Design dials: variance 8/10, motion 7/10, density 4/10.
Self-hosted fonts: Space Grotesk and IBM Plex Mono.
Dark palette: #0a0a0c / #f3f2ee / #ff864d.
Light palette: #f3f1ec / #171719 / #b93d08.

Technical reference: https://threejs.org/docs/pages/WebGLRenderer.html

## Generation prompts

All artwork is original, landscape 3:2, with no UI, lettering, logos or fake dashboards.

### public/art/chrome.webp

Polished silver chrome tubular knot suspended in a near-black studio. Sparse warm orange points and one fine filament. Center-right sculpture and negative space on the left. Editorial 3D macro, soft strip-light reflections, physical metal. Palette #09090b, #ff7439, silver. No blue, purple, or space nebula.

### public/art/dialogue.webp

Two rippled polished chrome spheres facing one another, connected by a delicate warm orange filament. Near-black studio, restrained silver and amber reflections, generous negative space. Abstract dialogue between agents. Editorial sculptural lighting.

### public/art/attractor.webp

Mathematical strange attractor. Hundreds of fine amber filaments form a double vortex on black, with silver-white highlights. Elegant long-exposure light sculpture, detailed but uncluttered.

### public/portrait.webp

Existing user photo PhotoProfile.jpg, rotated according to EXIF and resized to 720px width. No generated identity or changes to the person.
